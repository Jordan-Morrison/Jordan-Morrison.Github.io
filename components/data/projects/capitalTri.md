[Capital Tri](https://capitaltri.ca) is a triathlon coaching website I designed and developed from the ground up using Next.js, taking the project from initial concept through design, development and production deployment. Creating the complete visual identity and user experience went hand-in-hand with building a reusable component system and fully responsive layouts, providing a consistent experience across devices. Accessibility was a major focus throughout development, with Lighthouse testing used alongside optimized imagery and modern web development practices to ensure a fast, accessible and polished experience for everyone.
